Precision Engineered
MDF Panels

MDF (Medium Density Fibreboard) is an engineered wood product made from fine wood fibers bonded with resin under high temperature and pressure. Known for its smooth surface and excellent machinability, MDF is ideal for furniture, interior paneling, and decorative applications. It offers consistent strength, durability, and a flawless finish for painting and laminating.

KEY FEATURES

Exceptional Strength & Flawless Machinability

Carefully formulated with fine wood fibers and premium synthetic resin under continuous pressing for uncompromised architectural luxury.

Waterproof & Fireproof

Formulated with advanced moisture-resistant chemistry and fire-retardant additives, ensuring high performance in diverse Indian climatic conditions.

Resistant Against Pathogens

Inherent resistance against mold, fungi, bacterial proliferation, termites, and wood borers, preserving pristine interior hygiene.

Wide Applications

From fluted louvers and acoustic wall paneling to modular kitchens, embossed wardrobe doors, and high-gloss PU lacquered millwork.

Durability & Precision

Ultra-fine defibrated fibers allow smooth CNC cutting, profiling, beveling, and deep grooving with razor-sharp edge definition without chipping.

Sustainable & Safe

Certified low formaldehyde emission (E0/E1 compliant), non-toxic, and 100% lead-free, ensuring pure indoor air quality in living and commercial spaces.
