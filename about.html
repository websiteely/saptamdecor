AGRO-WOOD RESPONSIBILITY

Preserving Forests,
Upcycling Agro Biomass

Traditional wood panels deplete virgin timber forests. Saptam Decor pioneers a circular model that utilizes sugarcane bagasse fibers and fast-growing farm eucalyptus. We turn agricultural byproducts into architectural-grade engineered boards.

Discover Our Materials
✓
Zero Agricultural Stubble Burning:

Procuring regional sugarcane bagasse directly from Uttar Pradesh mills prevents seasonal open-field biomass burning.

✓
Low Formaldehyde Emission (E0/E1 Compliant):

Our thermosetting MUF resin formulations ensure superior indoor air quality for homes, schools, and healthcare environments.

✓
Energy-Efficient Thermopress Curing:

Closed-loop thermal fluid systems maximize heat recapture, lowering industrial carbon emissions by over 32% compared to conventional mills.

CHRONICLE OF GROWTH

Milestones of Excellence

From humble foundations in Muzaffarnagar to a national powerhouse in engineered architectural panels.

2018

Foundations Laid in Muzaffarnagar

Establishment of Saptam Decor Private Limited with a core mission: crafting pre-laminated bagasse particle boards from regional agro-residues.

2020

ISO 9001:2015 & BIS Certification

Secured international ISO 9001:2015 certification and Indian Standards (IS:3087) accreditation, confirming zero-defect calibrated density standards.

2023

SDHDMR & SD Boilo Commercialization

Launched Super Density SDHDMR and Boiling Waterproof SD Boilo (>1000 kg/m³ density) engineered specifically for high-humidity kitchens and washrooms.

2025–2026

High-Tech MDF Plant & Noida Tower

Commissioning state-of-the-art continuous MDF manufacturing capacity and inaugurating the corporate marketing headquarters at The I-Thum, Sector-62, Noida.
