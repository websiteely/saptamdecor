ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

The Anatomy of Resilience

Every Saptam panel is a symphony of multi-layer fusion bonding—engineered from raw agricultural fiber into an indestructible architectural standard.

01. Diamond Melamine Overlay
02. High-Definition Decor Foil
03. Super-Density Fibrous Core
04. Anti-Warp Moisture Barrier
LAYER 01

Diamond Melamine Wear Overlay

An optically transparent, crystalline overlay impregnated with specialized resins that shield against abrasive scratches, ultraviolet discoloration, and harsh chemical cleaning agents.

Scratch Resistance: Grade 4+ High Abrasion
UV Stability: Anti-Fading ISO 4586
LAYER 02

High-Definition Decor Foil

Printed with German high-fidelity pigments to mirror natural timber grains (5000 Series Oak, Walnut, Teak) and soft-touch contemporary architectural solids (1000 Series).

Print Quality: Synchronized Pore Texture
Color Fastness: Class 6 Blue Wool Standard
LAYER 03

Super-Density Bagasse & Agro-Wood Core

Manufactured from renewable sugarcane bagasse and plantation eucalyptus fibers, bonded under controlled high-temperature pressing into a dense, borer-proof, zero-cavity core.

Core Density: 750 to > 1000 kg/m³
Internal Bond: ≥ 0.85 N/mm²
LAYER 04

Balancing Moisture Barrier Film

A calibrated underlay film balancing the physical surface tensions, ensuring the board stays perfectly flat, dimensionally stable, and sealed against subterranean humidity.

Flatness Index: Zero Curvature Distortion
Water Vapor Barrier: 100% Sealed Base

OUR CHRONICLE

The Saptam Journey

From visionary foundation in 2018 to pioneering India's next-generation MDF facility in 2025/2026.

2018

Laying the Foundation

The vision for an agro-based eco-particle board plant took shape. Strategic plant location selected on Bhopa Road, Muzaffarnagar, followed by machinery procurement and regulatory approvals.

2019

Construction & Commissioning

Civil infrastructure completed, German and domestic precision machinery erected. Initial trial production runs executed to stabilize high-pressure bonding tolerances.

2020

Commercial Production Begins

Commercial operations inaugurated in January. Despite unprecedented pandemic headwinds, Saptam successfully created agile supply lines and introduced bagasse pre-laminated boards to North Indian distributors.

2021 - 2024

Market Expansion & Product Diversification

Broadened national footprint across architect and OEM networks. Introduced the SDHDMR and SD Boilo extreme-density product lines to solve damp-area cabinet decay.

2025 - 2026

High-Capacity Advanced MDF Venture

Launching our cutting-edge MDF production facility. Leveraging state-of-the-art continuous presses to provide complete interior solutions and solidify our ranking among India's top 3 wood-panel leaders.
