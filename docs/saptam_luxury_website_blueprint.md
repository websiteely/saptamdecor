1. **Interactive 3D Exploded Board Render (or Layered Video Scrub)**:

     1. *Layer 1: Melamine / Decorative Overlay (Scratch & UV Resistant)*
     2. *Layer 2: High-Definition Decor Foil (5000/4000/1000 Series Shades)*
     3. *Layer 3: Super Density Core (High-pressure compressed bagasse/wood fibers $> 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$)*

2. **Dynamic Physics Demonstrations (Scroll-Triggered Micro-Clips or WebGL)**:
